First, the honest part: we are not FEM simulation software

If what you need is coupled electromagnetic–thermal–structural simulation of transformer components — leakage-field mapping, local eddy and stray loss, hot-spot prediction from a meshed geometry — then you need a general-purpose finite-element package, and we are not it. We would rather tell you that now than after an invoice.

Because the question comes up constantly, we publish a vendor-neutral directory of the tools that do solve it, grouped by the job they do rather than by vendor. It covers the commercial packages (Ansys Maxwell, COMSOL, Simcenter MAGNET and Flux, Opera/CST, JMAG, EMWorks) and the free and open-source options — for 2D axisymmetric work, which covers most leakage-field, short-circuit-force and electrostatic-stress problems, FEMM is genuinely capable. We sell none of them and take no commission.

Our tools sit upstream of that, at the sizing and documentation stages where FEM is slow and overkill. Classical closed-form methods land within roughly 10–15 % of a works design — excellent for tender sizing, costing and sanity-checking a supplier's numbers, and not a substitute for FEM where the failure mode is local rather than average.
