Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026.

This policy explains what personal data TransformerPath ("we") collects, why, and your rights. TransformerPath is an information platform operated from Dubai, UAE, and is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your name (optional), email and chosen region.
  • Form submissions: details you provide via the contact, list/claim-company or sponsorship forms.
  • Usage data: anonymous, aggregated analytics to improve the site.

How we use it

  • To send the briefing you subscribed to (unsubscribe in one click from any email).
  • To respond to your enquiry or process a listing/sponsorship request.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it except with the service providers below who process it on our behalf.

Service providers

An email-marketing platform (subscriber lists + newsletter delivery), our website host (form submissions), and a privacy-friendly analytics provider. Each processes data only as needed to provide its service.

Cookies

We keep cookies to a minimum; essential cookies keep the site working, and any analytics are configured to be privacy-friendly and aggregated.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and unsubscribe at any time. To exercise any right, contact us. Depending on your location (UAE PDPL, EU/UK GDPR) you may have additional rights, including complaining to a supervisory authority.

Retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed and take reasonable measures to protect it. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Contact

Questions: contact us or email privacy@transformerpath.com.
