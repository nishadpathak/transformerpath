Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

This policy explains what personal data TransformerPath ("we") collects, why, and your rights. TransformerPath is an information platform operated by a freelancer under a freelance digital-marketing licence, and is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your name (optional), email and chosen region.
  • Form submissions: details you provide via the contact, list/claim-company, RFQ or sponsorship forms.
  • Usage and analytics data: we use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregated visitor interactions (pages visited, session duration, device category, approximate country). IP addresses are anonymized by default.
  • Advertising & geolocation data: when you allow advertising cookies, Google AdSense may collect cookie and device data to serve and measure ads. Some pages (Daily Intel, Events) also query your approximate country from your IP address so we can show the right regional content.

How we use it

  • To send the briefing you subscribed to (unsubscribe in one click from any email).
  • To respond to your enquiry or process a listing/sponsorship request.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it except with the service providers below who process it on our behalf.

Service providers

An email-marketing platform (subscriber lists + newsletter delivery), our website host Netlify (form submissions + hosting), Google (Google Analytics for aggregated site metrics and Google AdSense for advertising, both operating under Google's Privacy & Terms), and ipapi.co (an approximate country lookup from your IP address for regional content). Each processes data only as needed to provide its service.

Cookies & Analytics

Essential cookies keep the site working (theme preference, course-access state). Google Analytics (GA4) uses measurement cookies to help us evaluate site traffic and user engagement. You can opt out of Google Analytics measurement via the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on. We only load Google AdSense, which uses advertising cookies, after you accept our consent banner. If you choose "Essential only", AdSense is never loaded. You can also control personalised ads via Google Ads Settings and the YourAdChoices opt-out.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and unsubscribe at any time. To exercise any right, contact us. Depending on your location (e.g. EU/UK GDPR and other applicable data-privacy laws) you may have additional rights, including complaining to a supervisory authority.

Retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed and take reasonable measures to protect it. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Contact

TransformerPath is operated by the licence holder. Questions or data-requests: email privacy@transformerpath.com, or contact us.

This policy reflects how TransformerPath operates today and will be updated as the service evolves.

Payment processing

Where you make a purchase, payment is processed by Stripe, acting as our payment processor. Stripe receives your name and billing details to process your transaction; we do not store your card number. You may review Stripe's own privacy terms for its handling of that data.
