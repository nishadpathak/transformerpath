Would you sign off this FAT?

You're the buyer's witness reviewing the Factory Acceptance Test package for a 10 MVA transformer. The cover sheet says the tests passed. Can you trace each result to the unit on your purchase order?

  • About 5 minutes

Read the evidence packet

02 / Decide

What would you put in your witness note?

Choose the next step for this fictional inspection plan. You can change your answer at any time. Nothing is saved or submitted.

Choose a disposition

Read the witness reasoning and sample note

Hold signoff while traceability is unresolved

The unit nameplate and the FAT cover show SIM-26-0417. Sheet R-03 shows SIM-26-0471. The matching rating and recorded connection do not tie that sheet to this unit. The mismatch proves neither an electrical failure nor a pass for the ordered unit.

Record the mismatch and ask the manufacturer's quality team to reconcile the report with its original unit-linked records. Review a controlled correction or other verified evidence for the correct serial through the project’s agreed process. Do not edit the record yourself or assume it was a typo.

Sample witness note

PO SIM-PO-2417, item 010: hold FAT signoff pending reconciliation of sheet R-03. Unit and cover show SIM-26-0417; R-03 shows SIM-26-0471. Please provide verified, unit-linked evidence for SIM-26-0417 and the applicable controlled report revision. This is an open traceability issue, not a finding of electrical failure.

03 / Take it to work

Use the project's own requirements

This training case is not a test procedure, a standards reproduction or authorization to accept or release equipment. On a real project, follow the agreed purchase order, inspection plan, applicable standards and authorized signoff process.
