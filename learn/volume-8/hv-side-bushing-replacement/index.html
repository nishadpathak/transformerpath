HV side bushing replacement

Replacing the HV bushing on a liquid-immersed distribution or small power transformer of about 2 MVA. The outline is on this page. The procedure is the Learning plan.

Volume 8 — Bushings, taps, cooling. Written to IEC practice, with IEEE notes where they help. The transformer manual, the bushing installation sheet, and the site safety rules govern the job. This page does not offer a class or a call. Questions: hello@transformerpath.com.

Outline

  1. Safety, isolation, earthing, permits, and lock-out.
  2. Oil level and oil handling, including conservator and sealed tanks.
  3. Draw-lead and bottom-connected bushings, and porcelain versus condenser types at this rating.
  4. Removal.
  5. Gaskets and seals.
  6. Lead connection and torque.
  7. Refitting, venting, oil top-up, and standing time.
  8. Checks before re-energising.
  9. Checklist and a short FAQ.

The full lesson covers each item for an HV bushing change. It does not cover an LV bushing, a dry-type terminal, or a bushing above this class of transformer.
