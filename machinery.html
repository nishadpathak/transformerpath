400 kV / EHV Power Transformer Plant: Process & Machinery Roadmap

Setting up an EHV (400 kV–765 kV) power transformer plant requires a coordinated chain of specialized manufacturing equipment across 6 core shop-floor processes:

1. Core Slitting & Step-Lap Cutting
CNC step-lap cut-to-length lines with robotic stacking for 0.23–0.30 mm Hi-B CRGO steel sheet.
Key Builders: Heinrich Georg, Tuboly-Astronic, Soenen
2. High-Precision Coil Winding
Vertical & horizontal winding machines with continuous tension control for CTC and disk/helical windings.
Key Builders: Tuboly-Astronic, L&F, Joyee, BR Technology
3. CNC Solid Insulation Fabrication
5-axis CNC machining centers for transformerboard cylinders, angle rings, snout exits, and lead supports.
Key Builders: Weidmann, Krempel, Hedrich CNC
4. Vapour-Phase Drying (VPD) Autoclaves
Kerosene vapor-phase drying chambers ensuring active-part cellulose moisture is reduced to <0.5%.
Key Builders: Hedrich Group, Meier Prozesstechnik, Micafil
5. Vacuum Oil Degassing & Tank Filling
Multi-stage high-vacuum oil dehydration, degasification (<5 ppm moisture, <0.1% gas), and automated filling.
Key Builders: Micafil, Enervac, Filtervac, GlobeCore, Baron USA
6. High-Voltage Test Bay Systems
Faraday-shielded test bay with lightning/switching impulse generators (≥2,400 kV), PD detectors, and loss measurement.
Key Builders: Haefely, HIGHVOLT Dresden, Phenix Technologies
