About TrafoPath

The transformer industry, made navigable.

TrafoPath is an independent information platform for the global electrical-transformer industry — daily market intelligence, a worldwide manufacturer directory, a country-by-country electrical-grid census, an industry events calendar, and practical engineering tools and learning material, in one place.

Why it exists

The transformer sector is large, global and fast-moving — tenders, capacity shifts, material prices and grid investment change constantly — yet the information is scattered across dozens of sources and languages. TrafoPath gathers it, verifies it, and presents it clearly, so buyers, utilities, EPCs, engineers and suppliers can stay ahead without hunting.

Editorial independence

TrafoPath is independent and vendor-neutral. Directory entries are compiled from public sources and, where confirmed, verified by the companies themselves; our verification standards explain what each tier means. Sponsorships and advertising are always clearly identified and never influence the intelligence we publish.

Who runs it

TrafoPath is operated from Dubai, UAE. It is an information and media platform — not a broker, trader or party to any transaction. Always carry out your own due diligence before making commercial decisions.
