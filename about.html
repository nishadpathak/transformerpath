About TransformerPath

Independent intelligence, engineering and professional learning for the global transformer industry.

TransformerPath is an independent global platform dedicated to the transformer industry, bringing together market intelligence, engineering resources, manufacturer and supplier data, professional learning, events, and sourcing tools.

Why it exists

The transformer sector is large, global and fast-moving — tenders, capacity shifts, material prices and grid investment change constantly — yet the information is scattered across dozens of sources and languages. TransformerPath gathers it, verifies it, and presents it clearly, so buyers, utilities, EPCs, engineers and suppliers can stay ahead without hunting.

Editorial Independence

TransformerPath operates independently. Directory inclusion, verification, editorial coverage, rankings, technical content and commercial opportunities are governed by TransformerPath's published methodology and are not influenced by outside commercial relationships.

Standards references. References to IEC, IEEE and other standards identify technical frameworks used for engineering guidance. TransformerPath is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, these standards organisations. Users should consult the current official standards for contractual or compliance purposes.

Editorial & engineering review

Engineering content follows the IEC 60076 and IEEE C57 families, and methods are checked against the invoked standards and against practical works practice. Design calculations use classical closed-form methods: where a result depends on local or high-frequency effects this is noted and the text points to where finite-element analysis is required (see the analytical vs FEM boundary). The verification standards describe how directory entries are tiered, and correction policy is simple: errors are fixed as soon as they are confirmed.

Regarding the tools. The calculators, generators and checks are engineering aids for first-pass sizing and documentation. Outputs must be checked against the relevant published standard and a qualified engineer's judgement before they are relied upon.

Not a broker or party to transactions

TransformerPath is an information and media platform — not a broker, trader or party to any transaction. Always carry out your own due diligence before making commercial decisions.
