Design support, cohort and consulting

Three ways to work with TransformerPath engineers: a design-office block, a live cohort, and a consulting engagement. Each one is scoped in writing before work starts. This page is separate from the service and repair directory.

Design-office support

Remote review of a design package: ratings, impedance, and the questions a buyer will ask. The reply is in writing.

Live cohort

A scheduled live course for a small group, taught by TransformerPath. Dates are set when a cohort is confirmed.

Consulting

A fixed engagement for one defined question, with the deliverable agreed before the work starts.
