Saudi Electricity Co / National Grid SA

Country Saudi ArabiaTop voltage 380 kVRegion Middle EastOfficial site ↗

Single national grid — only 60 Hz system in GCC, tied to GCCIA via HVDC back-to-back at Al-Fadhili

Overview

The Saudi Electricity Company (SEC) and its National Grid operating arm — the dominant buyer of 380/110 kV power transformers, reactors and GSUs across the Kingdom under Vision 2030.

Grid characteristics

  • Operating region: Saudi Arabia — Middle East
  • System frequency: 60 Hz
  • Synchronous area: GCCIA Interconnection
  • Top voltage class: 380 kV

Transformer & substation profile

  • Highest voltage class in this market: 380 kV
  • Transformer demand driver: Single national grid — only 60 Hz system in GCC, tied to GCCIA via HVDC back-to-back at Al-Fadhili
  • Procurement notes: Utilities in this market procure against national grid codes and international standards — verify the governing standard before a tender.
