Click any part to identify it. A GSU is a two-winding power transformer: the generator feeds the low-voltage delta; the star HV (neutral earthed) feeds the transmission line.
🖱 drag = orbit · wheel = zoom · right-drag = pan · click = identify
Delta LV (generator, high current) → star HV (line). The generator's AVR regulates voltage, so a GSU usually has only off-circuit taps.
