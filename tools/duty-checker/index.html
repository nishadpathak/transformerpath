Duty checker

The first module estimates one EU provisional safeguard: the declared core weight inside a covered imported transformer.

This is an estimate only, not customs advice. TransformerPath is not a customs broker. Confirm the code, the origin, and the declared core weight before you rely on a figure.

EU core-duty estimator

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2133 of 18 September 2026 sets a provisional safeguard duty of EUR 1,140 per tonne of the core incorporated in a covered transformer. The rate uses the declared core weight. It does not use the weight of the whole unit, and it does not apply to all electrical steel.

Loose grain-oriented steel (CN 7225 11 00 and 7226 11 00) and standalone laminations or cores (CN 8504 90 13) follow a price-threshold and quota rule. This module does not compute those with the EUR 1,140 rate.

The regulation is dated 18 September 2026 and enters into force on 25 September 2026. 26 February 2027 is the provisional end date of the measure. It is not a confirmed decision date. Source: OJ L 2026/2133 on EUR-Lex.

Eight-digit CN, or a six-digit heading only when the regulation lists one CN line under it.

1.5 and 1,5 are the same. 1,500 is one thousand five hundred. 1.500 asks you to confirm which you mean.

When this module shows a number

A number is the declared core tonnes multiplied by EUR 1,140. Article 1 applies that rate to imports into the Union under the transformer CN codes in Article 1(3). Article 5 excludes Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Kenya, and Ukraine. Annex III excludes other developing-country origins, except China, Türkiye, and the United Arab Emirates for laminations and cores inside transformers. A Union origin is not an import into the Union, so this rate is not charged.

An excluded origin, a Union origin, a code for loose steel or a standalone core, a code that is not one of those transformer lines, or an origin this page does not recognise, returns Check with your broker. No duty figure is shown in those cases.
