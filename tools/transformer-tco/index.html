How the estimate works

Annual no-load energy: no-load loss (kW) × energized hours.

Annual load-loss energy: full-load loss (kW) × (equivalent load % ÷ 100)² × energized hours.

Modeled total cost: upfront price + the lifetime value of both electricity losses.

For a variable load, take the square root of the time-weighted mean of the squared load fractions, counting hours at zero load if energized. For example, equal energized time at 0% and 100% is about 70.7% RMS, not 50%. The load-loss model approximates operation at the quoted test basis; it does not correct losses for operating temperature or harmonics.

The same duty and energy tariff are repeated each year. The purchase price is at time zero. A non-zero real discount rate discounts each year's loss cost from year end to today. The tariff is constant in today's money; no inflation or escalation is added.

What this does not include

Exclude demand charges, cooling or auxiliary power not included in the quoted losses, maintenance, outages, disposal, residual value and installation or other costs unless explicitly and consistently included in your upfront prices. Do not use this comparison as a compliance, equipment sizing or design verification tool. It does not calculate or certify test losses.
