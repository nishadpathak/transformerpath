Distribution

Lead times for transformer orders, particularly distribution transformers, increased from 3 to 6 months in 2019 to 12 to 30 months in 2023. The same article says utility trade members reported that, between 2020 and 2022, orders which took 2 to 4 months then took 22 to 33 months.

U.S. DOE Office of Electricity (22 Feb 2024)

Pad-mount distribution lead times reached about 75 weeks after receipt of order, on average, over the two quarters before the article. The article states no week count for pole-top units.

Large power / GSU

Power and generator step-up lead times reached about 150 weeks after receipt of order, on average, over the two quarters before the article.

Wood Mackenzie (27 Jun 2024)

For a large power transformer of 100 MVA or greater, 36-month lead times are commonly quoted and maximum lead times reach as much as 60 months.

U.S. Department of Energy (July 2024)

Generator step-up lead times eased to 143 weeks on average in the quarter the article calls Q2. The same article says the U.S. market had fallen into a deficit, with 2 to 3 year lead times for these units.

Wood Mackenzie (15 Oct 2025)

Large power transformer lead times currently range from 48 to 60 months.

CWIEME Berlin (17 Mar 2026)

Large power transformer lead times average around 12 months.

Procurement takes up to four years for large power transformers, and the survey says that wait is twice as long as in 2021.

International Energy Agency (25 Feb 2025)

Large substation power and generator step-up transformers have lead times ranging from 80 to 210 weeks.

Wood Mackenzie (2 Apr 2024)

Figures not placed in a cell

  • Wood Mackenzie, 2 April 2024, states an all-transformer average of about 120 weeks in 2024, from about 50 weeks in 2021. That sentence is not limited to one of the three classes. The same article's 80 to 210 week range for large substation power and generator step-up units is in the table.
  • Wood Mackenzie, 15 October 2025, states a 128-week average for power transformers in the quarter it calls Q2. The article does not say whether that average is medium power or large power. The same article's generator step-up figures are in the United States large-power cell.
  • CWIEME Berlin, 17 March 2026, says distribution lead times peaked above 100 weeks in 2023 and have since eased. It does not state the eased figure or a region.
  • Medium power has no duration in these sources. Middle East, Africa, Latin America, and Oceania have no duration for any of the three classes in these sources.
