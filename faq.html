Frequently asked questions

What TransformerPath is, what's free, what the plans include, and how the tools and data are maintained.

What is TransformerPath?

TransformerPath is the transformer industry platform — daily market intelligence, a worldwide manufacturer & supplier directory, an electrical-grid census, a transformer/electrical events & webinar directory, and hands-on engineering tools (the IEC 60076 design calculator, rating-plate and test-report generators).

Is it free?

Yes — the core is free forever: Daily Intel, the basic manufacturer & supplier directories, events, webinars, grids, industry news, knowledge articles, job browsing, RFQ submission and a selection of engineering calculators and book excerpts.

What do the plans cost?

Learning $199, Professional $599 and Team $1,999 — each a single payment for 12 months of access, with no auto-renewal. If checkout is paused, email hello@transformerpath.com.

What does the design calculator do?

A live analytical (first-pass) IEC 60076 transformer design calculator for distribution, power and cast-resin modes: rating plate, core & winding sizing, turns, conductors, losses & efficiency, leakage reactance, a short-circuit thermal screening reference (analytical, per-winding under stated assumptions — NOT a verified or standalone design check), impulse / BIL levels, a to-scale cross-section drawing, and a downloadable PDF design report plus a BOM (Excel). Results are first-cut/tender-grade; check against the current published standard before reliance.

Do I get the books?

The TransformerPath Professional Engineering Series (V1, V2) is sold separately — digital Professional Edition and a Premium printed edition — and can be bundled with a membership. Book chapters link to the Academy lessons and the interactive 3D models.

How is the manufacturer & supplier data sourced and structured?

TransformerPath maintains a strict 4-tier supplier state architecture:

  • Listed — Baseline public-source profile created by TransformerPath from manufacturer documentation, official filings, and utility registries.
  • Claimed — Authorized company representative has taken control/ownership of the profile to manage factual listings.
  • Verified — Company identity, registration, and published contact/profile details have passed TransformerPath verification.
  • Supplier Pro — Paid commercial functionality (RFQ management, priority matching, digital catalogue distribution, market analytics). Supplier Pro is a commercial tier, not a higher technical verification level.

Verification does NOT mean: product certification, factory approval, financial verification, utility approval, quality guarantee, technical endorsement, or a TransformerPath recommendation.

How fresh is Daily Intel?

The curated Daily Intel feed displays the date of the last successful data refresh. When the refresh occurred within 36 hours, it is indicated as Data Current; 36 to 72 hours is Refresh Delayed; beyond 72 hours is Data Stale. Page build date and source data refresh date are distinct.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse — the core is free without signing in. An account lets engineers keep saved designs, bookmarks, notes and follows in one workspace across devices. The account layer is in beta: sign up from your workspace to try it.

How do I subscribe or pay?

Via the Learning/Professional/Team buttons on the pricing page (Stripe checkout). If payments are temporarily paused while links are refreshed, email hello@transformerpath.com and we'll send the checkout link.

Do you issue a certificate of completion?

No — TransformerPath is not an accreditation body; it issues course completion records for its learning tracks. These are not accredited qualifications, professional certifications, licences, or authorisations to practise engineering. Academy courses and capstones are manually reviewed, and each completion record carries a unique verification page.

Can my company be listed as a supplier?

Yes. All manufacturers begin as Listed profiles. Company representatives can claim their profile, complete Verified identity status, or upgrade to Supplier Pro for commercial capabilities including RFQ management, digital catalogue distribution, and market analytics.

Which standards do you use?

Engineering content and tools follow IEC 60076 (and IEEE/ANSI C57 where relevant). Methodology, assumptions and limitations are documented on each calculator; results are a first-cut/tender-grade design, not a final factory production design.
