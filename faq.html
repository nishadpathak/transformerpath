Frequently asked questions

What TransformerPath is, what's free, what the plans include, and how the tools and data are maintained.

What is TransformerPath?

TransformerPath is the transformer industry platform — daily market intelligence, a worldwide manufacturer & supplier directory, an electrical-grid census, a transformer/electrical events & webinar directory, and hands-on engineering tools (the IEC 60076 design calculator, rating-plate and test-report generators).

Is it free?

Yes — the core is free forever: Daily Intel, the basic manufacturer & supplier directories, events, webinars, grids, industry news, knowledge articles, job browsing, RFQ submission and a selection of engineering calculators and book excerpts.

What do the plans cost?

Engineer $199/year, Professional $599/year and Enterprise $1,999/year. Each is a single payment for 12 months of access — there is currently no auto-renewal. If checkout is paused, email hello@transformerpath.com.

What does the design calculator do?

A live IEC 60076 transformer design calculator for distribution, power and cast-resin modes: rating plate, core & winding sizing, turns, conductors, losses & efficiency, leakage reactance, short-circuit thermal withstand (IEC 60076-5), impulse / BIL levels, a to-scale cross-section drawing, and a downloadable PDF design report plus a BOM (Excel).

Do I get the books?

The TransformerPath Professional Engineering Series (V1, V2) is sold separately — digital Professional Edition and a Premium printed edition — and can be bundled with a membership. Book chapters link to the Academy lessons and the interactive 3D models.

How is the manufacturer & supplier data sourced?

Profiles are TransformerPath-created from public sources (verified listings). Companies can claim and verify their profile — Verified and Pro levels confirm the information, and we make clear we do not guarantee product quality.

How fresh is Daily Intel?

Updated twice daily at 06:00 and 18:00 UTC, free, with an RSS feed. Each item links to its original source, and the paper is region-filterable.

Do I need an account?

Not today. Accounts are being added so engineers can keep saved designs, bookmarks, notes and follows in one workspace — you'll be able to sign up and use it on any device.

How do I subscribe or pay?

Via the Engineer/Professional/Enterprise buttons on the pricing page (Stripe checkout). If payments are temporarily paused while links are refreshed, email hello@transformerpath.com and we'll send the checkout link.

Do you provide professional certification?

No — we do not issue accredited or licensed professional credentials. Academy courses and capstones are engineer-reviewed, and we issue certificates of completion with a unique verification page, clearly distinct from a license or certification.

Can my company be listed as a supplier?

Yes. Suppliers can take a Verified / Pro / Premium tier to control their profile, publish a catalogue, get enhanced search placement, analytics and RFQ eligibility. Claim your profile from its listing page.

Which standards do you use?

Engineering content and tools follow IEC 60076 (and IEEE/ANSI C57 where relevant). Methodology, assumptions and limitations are documented on each calculator; results are a first-cut/tender-grade design, not a final works design.
