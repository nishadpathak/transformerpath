Power Transformer — Component Overview

This is the non-3D accessible version of the Transformer Lab.

  1. External Accessories — conservator, Buchholz relay, surge arresters
  2. Cooling System — radiator banks and fans (ONAN/ONAF). Oil pumps only if an OFAF rating is specified
  3. HV Bushings — 132 kV condenser-graded bushings, bushing CTs
  4. LV Bushings — 33 kV porcelain bushings, neutral bushing
  5. Tank — welded plate tank, cover, pressure-relief valves
  6. Active Part — core + windings + insulation assembly
  7. OLTC — on-load tap-changer and motor drive
  8. Clamping Structure — top yoke, tie rods, clamping frames
  9. Tapping Winding — outermost regulating winding
  10. HV Winding — 132 kV continuous disc winding
  11. Insulation Barriers — pressboard cylinders, HV–LV barrier
  12. LV Winding — 33 kV helical CTC winding
  13. Core — three-limb Hi-B CRGO core, limbs and bottom yoke

Your browser or device does not support WebGL. Here is the full disassembly sequence as a text overview.

  1. Complete transformer — all subsystems assembled
  2. Remove surge arresters
  3. Remove conservator, Buchholz relay and accessories
  4. Remove radiator banks and cooling plant
  5. Remove 132 kV HV bushings
  6. Remove 33 kV LV bushings and neutral bushing
  7. Remove tank cover, PRVs and inspection hatches
  8. X-ray: active part revealed inside transparent tank
  9. Lift active part clear of tank
  10. Remove tank and oil — active part isolated
  11. Isolate OLTC
  12. Separate top yoke and clamping structure
  13. Separate outermost tapping winding
  14. Separate HV winding and HV–tap barrier
  15. Separate LV winding and main HV–LV barrier
  16. Core exposed — limbs and bottom yoke
  17. Fully exploded engineering view
