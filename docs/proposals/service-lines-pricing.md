## Arithmetic behind the estimates

Z Industries publishes USD 2,400 as a day rate for a power-quality study. Design-office support at USD 4,800 is 2 × 2,400. Consulting at USD 12,000 is 5 × 2,400. Both are estimates because that day rate is for a power-quality study, and the same page says design work is quoted for complexity.

Cross-check, also calculated rather than published as a day rate: IPE’s licensed-engineer floor is USD 250 per hour and the sheet’s minimum day is 8 hours, including remote work. 250 × 8 = USD 2,000 for a day at the floor. Two such days would be USD 4,000. The proposal uses Z Industries’ published day figure instead of this product.

The live-cohort seat price is not multiplied. DNV’s EUR 3,500 is the closest published syllabus (design, specification, and testing over four days) if a euro price is preferred later. Coursera Plus at USD 59 per month and the IEEE Transformers Committee member registration at USD 495 are published, and they are the wrong product for a live specialist cohort.
