Transformer Dielectric Fluids & Esters

Inhibited Mineral Insulating Oil, Natural Esters (FR3) & Synthetic Esters (MIDEL)

Governing Standards: IEC 60296 IEC 62770 IEEE C57.147 IEC 61099

Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Fluid Types Inhibited / Uninhibited Mineral Oil, Natural Ester, Synthetic Ester
Breakdown Voltage ≥ 70 kV (processed oil at 2.5 mm gap per IEC 60156)
Fire Point (Esters) > 300°C (K-Class high fire point per IEC 61039)
Biodegradability > 95% within 28 days for natural ester fluids (OECD 301)
Kinematic Viscosity ≤ 11 mm²/s at 40°C (standard mineral oil)

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

Severe Hydrotreated Mineral Oil

High oxidation stability, low pour point (-40°C), optimized for maximum cooling velocity.

Natural Ester (K-Class)

Seed oil based fluid offering self-extinguishing fire safety, 100% biodegradability, and extended paper life.

Synthetic Organic Ester

Pentaerythritol ester designed for sub-zero climates (-56°C pour point), high moisture tolerance, and wind/rail applications.

Regenerated & Re-refined Oils

Circular economy transformer fluids reducing carbon footprint with full IEC 60296 compliance.
