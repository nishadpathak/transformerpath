Continuously Transposed Conductors (CTC)

Enamelled, Epoxy-Bonded & Thermally Upgraded Copper CTC for Power Transformers

Governing Standards: IEC 60317-28 IEC 60851 ASTM B48 BS EN 13601
Industry Knowledge Graph Route
Product › Supplier › Factory › Country › Direct RFQ

Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Conductor Material Oxygen-Free High Conductivity Copper (Cu-OF / Cu-ETP ≥ 99.99%)
Number of Strands 5 to 83 rectangular enamelled strands
Enamel Coating Polyvinyl Formal (PVF/Formvar) or Polyesterimide + Polyamideimide (Theic)
Bonding Layer Epoxy resin coated (cures during drying to withstand short-circuit radial forces)
Paper Wrapping Pure kraft paper, thermally upgraded paper, or aramid (Nomex)

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

Standard Kraft Paper CTC

Individual enamelled strips continuously transposed to minimize eddy-current circulation losses in high-current windings.

Epoxy-Bonded CTC

Special thermo-setting adhesive bond curing during vacuum vapor-phase heating into an exceptionally rigid beam.

Netless CTC

Direct strand interlock without internal polyester mesh to maximize copper packing factor.

High-Temperature Aramid CTC

Insulated with DuPont Nomex for hybrid dry-type and high-overload traction transformers.
