Transformer Pressboard & Cellulose Insulation

Pre-Compressed Transformerboard, Laminated Wood, Crepe Paper & Machined Kits

Governing Standards: IEC 60641 IEC 60763 ASTM D4063 DIN 7707

Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Pulp Base 100% unbleached softwood sulphate chemical pulp
Density Range 0.90 to 1.30 g/cm³ (pre-compressed)
Dielectric Strength ≥ 45 kV/mm (oil-impregnated at 90°C)
Moisture Content ≤ 6.0% as delivered, dried to ≤ 0.5% during factory vacuum vapor phase
Ash Content ≤ 0.70% (high chemical purity)

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

Pre-Compressed Board (B.3.1)

High-density, dimensionally stable pressboard for clamping rings, pressure rings, and high-voltage barriers.

Calendered Board (B.2.1)

Flexible, moldable pressboard for cylinders, wrap insulation, and duct strips.

Laminated Wood (Lignostone)

Vacuum-densified beechwood with synthetic resin bonding for mechanical coil clamping rings and beam supports.

Diamond Dotted Paper (DDP)

Kraft paper coated with epoxy resin dots on both sides for layer-insulated distribution windings.

CNC Insulation Kits

Prefabricated insulation packages: molded angle rings, snouts, lead exits, and stepped spacers.
