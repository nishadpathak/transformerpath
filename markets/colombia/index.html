Frequently asked

What voltage does Colombia use?

The Colombian grid is 500 kV and 230 kV transmission, with 115 kV, 34.5 kV and 13.2 kV distribution, at 60 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Colombia?

ISA and the transmission companies, the distributors and the renewable/industrial developers.

Is Colombia a notable market?

Yes — a hydro-dominated Latin American grid with steady transformer and substation demand.
