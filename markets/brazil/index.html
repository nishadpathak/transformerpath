Technical context

  • 500 kV / 230 / 138 / 88 / 69 kV transmission; 60 Hz
  • ONS (Operador Nacional do Sistema) grid codes and interconnection rules
  • A large domestic power/distribution transformer OEM base
  • Hydro-dominated generation plus growing wind and solar build-out
  • Transmission auctions (leilões) drive substation and transformer packages

Frequently asked

What voltage does Brazil transmit at?

The SIN operates at 500 kV and 230 kV transmission, with 138 kV, 88 kV and 69 kV sub-transmission and lower distribution voltages, at 60 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Brazil?

Transmission utilities (cemig, Taesa, ISA, Eletrobras companies) and distribution utilities, plus the winning consortia of the transmission auctions.

Is Brazil a major transformer producer?

Yes — Brazil has a substantial domestic power and distribution transformer industry serving both its own grid and export markets.
