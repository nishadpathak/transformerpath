🇨🇦 Transformer Industry — Canada

Canada is a 60 Hz North American market with large hydro, a strong grid and growing demand from electrification, renewables and data centres. Provincial utilities (Hydro-Québec, BC Hydro, etc.) buy transformers to ANSI/IEEE standards, and the country has a capable domestic OEM/component base.

Frequently asked

What voltage does Canada use?

Transmission runs at 765 kV, 500 kV, 315 kV and 230 kV, with 115 kV and lower distribution, at 60 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Canada?

The provincial utilities (Hydro-Québec, BC Hydro, Ontario, etc.) and renewable/industrial developers.

What standards apply?

Canadian utilities use ANSI/IEEE (C57) standards with local CSA requirements, at 60 Hz.
