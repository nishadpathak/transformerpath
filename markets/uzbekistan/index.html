Frequently asked

What voltage does Uzbekistan use?

The grid is 500 kV and 220 kV, with 110 kV and 10 kV distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Uzbekistan?

The national utility and regional electricity companies, plus the industrial and renewable developers.

Why is demand rising?

A major grid-renewal and industrial/renewable build-out programme is driving transformer and substation demand.
