Frequently asked

What voltage does Venezuela use?

The grid includes 765 kV and 400 kV transmission, with 230 kV, 138 kV and 34.5/13.8 kV distribution, at 60 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Venezuela?

Corpoelec and the regional electricity companies are the main buyers.

What is driving demand?

Asset renewal and grid modernisation after years of under-investment are the key transformer and substation needs.
