Frequently asked

What voltage does Bulgaria use?

The ESO grid is 400 kV and 220 kV, with 110 kV and 20 kV distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Bulgaria?

ESO (transmission), the distribution companies and the renewable/industrial developers.

Is Bulgaria a manufacturing hub?

Yes — Bulgaria has a notable transformer and components manufacturing base and is an established European sourcing hub.
