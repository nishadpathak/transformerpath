🇳🇱 Transformer Industry — Netherlands

The Netherlands operates a dense 50 Hz ENTSO-E grid under TenneT (transmission) and regional DSOs, with strong offshore-wind, data-centre and electrification demand. Its transformer market is driven by offshore-wind export, grid reinforcement and a major import-reliance story.

Projects & intelligence

  • TenneT €1.9bn substation framework (Siemens, Hitachi, GE Grid, Royal SMIT)

    Framework covers 160 power transformers + 110 compensation coils for Germany (€1.16bn) and Netherlands (€730m). Multi-year, extendable — ongoing call-offs.

  • GE Vernova–Seatrium win TenneT North Sea HVDC grid connection

    Major contract to connect North Sea wind power to Germany's grid under TenneT's 2 GW programme; works commence from January 2026 — sustained European HV transformer pull.

  • Hitachi Energy delivers first low-carbon copper transformer to TenneT Germany

    380 MVA unit (Bad Honnef plant) installed at Hardebek near Hamburg — 18% lower embedded copper emissions; ~9,500 t CO₂ saved across the frame agreement. Bad Honnef expansion completes 2026.

Frequently asked

What voltage does the Netherlands use?

TenneT operates at 380 kV and 220 kV, with 150 kV, 110 kV and lower distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in the Netherlands?

TenneT (transmission), the DSOs and the offshore-wind and data-centre developers.

Why is demand growing?

Offshore-wind export, grid reinforcement and data-centre load are driving transformer demand.
