Frequently asked

What voltage does Vietnam use?

The national grid is 500 kV and 220 kV transmission with 110 kV, 35 kV and 22 kV distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Vietnam?

EVN and its regional power companies, plus industrial parks and the growing renewable and utility-scale solar/wind sector.

Does Vietnam manufacture transformers?

Yes — Vietnam has a substantial distribution and power transformer manufacturing base, including MBT, THIBIDI, EEMC (Dong Anh), BTH and HBT.
