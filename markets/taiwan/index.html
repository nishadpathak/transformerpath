Frequently asked

What voltage does Taiwan use?

The Taipower grid is 345 kV and 161 kV, with 69 kV and 22.8/11.4 kV distribution, at 60 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Taiwan?

Taipower (transmission and distribution), plus the industrial, data-centre and renewable developers.

Does Taiwan manufacture transformers?

Yes — Taiwan has a capable base including Fortune Electric, Shihlin Electric, TECO and Allis Electric.
