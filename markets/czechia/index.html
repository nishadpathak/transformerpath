Frequently asked

What voltage does Czechia use?

ČEPS operates at 400 kV and 220 kV, with 110 kV and 22 kV distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Czechia?

ČEPS (transmission), the distribution companies and the industrial developers.

Is there a transformer OEM base?

Yes — Czechia has a strong industrial and component base and is an established European sourcing hub.
