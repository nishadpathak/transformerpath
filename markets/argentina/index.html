🇦🇷 Transformer Industry — Argentina

Argentina operates a 50 Hz grid under CAMMESA (system operator) and Transener (transmission), with a large domestic transformer manufacturing base and demand from grid renewal, renewables and the Vaca Muerta energy build-out. It is a significant Latin American producer and consumer.

Frequently asked

What voltage does Argentina use?

The grid is 500 kV and 330 kV transmission, with 220 kV, 132 kV and 66 kV distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in Argentina?

Transener (transmission), the distributors and the renewable/industrial developers, plus the Vaca Muerta energy build-out.

Does Argentina manufacture transformers?

Yes — Argentina has a large domestic power and distribution transformer manufacturing base serving both its grid and exports.
