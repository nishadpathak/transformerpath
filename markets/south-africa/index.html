Frequently asked

What voltage does South Africa use?

Eskom operates 765 kV, 400 kV and 275 kV transmission, with 132 kV, 88 kV, 66 kV and 22 kV distribution, at 50 Hz.

Who buys transformers in South Africa?

Eskom (transmission and generation) and the municipal distributors, plus renewable and industrial developers.

Why is demand growing?

Grid reinforcement, a large renewables build-out and the energy-transition programme are driving transformer, reactor and GSU demand.
