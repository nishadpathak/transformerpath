Suggest a Correction

Found something wrong on a transformer manufacturer profile? Tell us what and why, with a source where you can. Corrections are reviewed by the engineering and research team before that profile is changed.

How it works. TransformerPath profiles separate researched facts (factories, reported capability, approvals, projects) from company-provided information (description, catalogue, contacts, logos). A correction suggests a change to either; it is not applied automatically. We verify against a source before updating. Verified/paid listing status does not influence the research or editorial outcome of a correction.

Corrections are reviewed. A profile is never auto-overwritten; researched facts are only changed with a credible source.
