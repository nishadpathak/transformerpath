What a result means

Exact nominal fields
The public reference matches the requested kVA and listed winding values and connections. This is not a determination of fit.
Possible tap check
A source lists the requested primary voltage as an alternate tap, not as the nominal rating. Confirm against the actual nameplate, equipment limits and utility requirements. No tap actions are recommended here.
Incomplete source
Published values do not contradict the request, but at least one required field is missing. It is not an exact or tap match.

Before specifying equipment, verify frequency, connection and grounding, BIL, impedance, protection, front/feed arrangement, tests and delivery with qualified parties. A source-check date is not a stock check, confirmation of condition, dispatch estimate or certificate.
