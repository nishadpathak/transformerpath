Major Frameworks Comparison

IEC 60076 — IEC 60076 — Power transformers

International / adopted widely

IEC 60076 is the global series for power transformers: Part 1 (general), Part 2 (temperature rise), Part 3 (insulation levels & dielectric tests), Part 5 (ability to withstand short circuit), Part 7 (loading guide). Metric units, 50 or 60 Hz. Used across Europe, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and most of the Commonwealth.

Metric units (kV, MVA, °C)50 or 60 HzTemperature-rise and insulation levels in Part 2/3Most widely cross-border accepted series

IEEE C57 / ANSI — IEEE C57 (ANSI) — US/Canada

North America

The IEEE C57 series (adopted as ANSI) governs power & distribution transformers in the US and Canada: C57.12.00 (general), C57.12.10 (power transformer requirements), C57.12.90 (test code). Imperial-derived units, 60 Hz (tied to the North American 60 Hz grid).

60 Hz (North American grid)IEEE/ANSI C57 series + NEMA for distributionTest code in C57.12.90differing cooling & BIL conventions from IEC

BS EN — BS EN 60076 (UK) — EN adoption

United Kingdom / Europe

The UK adopts IEC 60076 as a British/European Standard (BS EN 60076), with BS EN 50341/EN additions for grid connection and a 400/275/132 kV transmission convention. 50 Hz.

BS EN 60076 = IEC 60076 as EN50 HzUK transmission 400 kV / 275 kV / 132 kVEN additions for connection & safety

IS 1180 — IS 1180 / IS 2026 (India)

India

India's distribution transformers follow IS 1180 and power transformers IS 2026 (aligned to IEC 60076), under the Central Electricity Authority and BIS. 50 Hz, 765/400/220/132/66/33/11 kV.

IS 1180 (distribution) / IS 2026 (power)50 Hz765 kV down to 11 kVBIS; CEA standards

GB — GB (China) — GB 1094

China

China's national standards align closely with IEC 60076 (GB 1094 for transformers) plus UHV practice: 1000 kV AC and ±800/±1100 kV DC, 750/500/220/110 kV. 50 Hz.

GB 1094 ≈ IEC 6007650 HzUHV AC to 1000 kV and DC ±1100 kV750/500/220/110 kV below

JIS — JIS C 4304 / JEC (Japan)

Japan

Japan uses JIS and JEC transformer standards; its grid is split 50/60 Hz (East/West) with 500/275/154/66/22 kV.

JIS C 4304 / JEC50 Hz (East) and 60 Hz (West)500 kV / 275 kV / 154 kV / 66 kVSeparate regional frequency zones
