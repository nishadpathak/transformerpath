Questions

Is this a subscription?

Learning is a single $199 payment for 12 months of access. Checkout runs through Stripe. Access is stored as an account entitlement (User → Purchase → Entitlement → Plan → Expiry) so you can sign in on another device. Stripe is the payment processor only.

Professional and Team are single payments. They unlock everything on that plan for 12 months with no auto-renewal — if you want to continue afterwards, you purchase again.

Which plan should I pick?

If you're learning on your own, Learning covers the masterclass, interactive 3D engineering models, TransformerPath Grid Lab, the calculator and the Design Engineer Track. Professional adds the entitlement-gated workspace tools (saved designs, BOM, PDF reports, rating plate, test report, country adapter). Manufacturer shortlist/compare in the Directory is free. Team is up to 10 people, each with their own account on one organization.

How do I access my plan after paying?

Create or sign in to a TransformerPath account (My TransformerPath). Checkout runs through Stripe; the webhook writes a Purchase and an Entitlement against that account. Sign in on any device to keep access — it is not a browser-only unlock.

How do Team seats work?

Team is an organization plan: up to 10 memberships. Each teammate signs in with their own account. There is no shared unlock link — a link is not a product.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes — purchase the higher plan any time and your access is extended and upgraded automatically; you keep whichever expiry date is later, you never lose time you've already paid for.

Does the design calculator send my inputs anywhere?

No. The IEC 60076 calculator runs entirely in your browser — there's no server call, no AI model in the loop, nothing logged on our side. Your inputs and your PDF exports never leave your device. You can verify this yourself: open your browser's network tab while you run a design and watch nothing go out.
