Questions

Is this a subscription?

Learning is a single $199 payment for 12 months of access. Checkout runs through Stripe; on return your access unlocks automatically in the browser you paid from, verified server-side. (If the checkout is temporarily unavailable the button safely falls back to an email request rather than charging you without a confirmable access path.)

Professional and Team are single payments. They unlock everything on that plan for 12 months with no auto-renewal — if you want to continue afterwards, you purchase again.

Which plan should I pick?

If you're learning on your own, Learning covers the entire course, the design calculator and the full Design Engineer Track — most people never need more than that. The public 3D explorers on the homepage are free for everyone. Professional is for anyone who wants faster capstone turnaround and first access to new material. Team is for a team of up to 10 sharing one plan.

How do I access my plan after paying?

Checkout runs through Stripe. When you return to TransformerPath, your access is unlocked automatically in the browser you paid from — no account or password needed. If you switch devices or clear your browser data, open this page from the device you checked out on, or contact us and we'll help you recover access.

How does the Team link work?

After purchase you'll receive one shared link — send it to up to 10 teammates and each of them unlocks the course in their own browser by opening it once. There's no per-seat account setup on our side.

Can I try the 3D explorer before paying?

Yes. The interactive construction explorer and the power-transformer 3D workstation are free to use — no account required. Paid Learning / Professional / Team plans unlock the design calculator, masterclass, Design Engineer Track, and the deeper academy 3D tools.

What is your refund policy?

Plans are single payments with no auto-renewal. You may request a refund within 14 days of purchase if the learning material was not substantially accessed — details are in Terms — Refunds & cancellation. Contact hello@transformerpath.com.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes — purchase the higher plan any time and your access is extended and upgraded automatically; you keep whichever expiry date is later, you never lose time you've already paid for.

Does the design calculator send my inputs anywhere?

No. The IEC 60076 calculator runs entirely in your browser — there's no server call, no AI model in the loop, nothing logged on our side. Your inputs and your PDF exports never leave your device. You can verify this yourself: open your browser's network tab while you run a design and watch nothing go out.
