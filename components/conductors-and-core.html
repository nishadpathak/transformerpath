Conductors & Magnetic Core Materials

Applicable Standards: IEC 60404-8-7, ASTM A876, IEC 60317

Continuously transposed conductors (CTC), enamelled magnet wire, oxygen-free copper and aluminium strip, Grain-Oriented Electrical Steel (CRGO), and amorphous alloy ribbons.

Technical Classifications

Continuously Transposed Conductors (CTC)

Multi-strand transposed copper strips with epoxy bonding and kraft/Netome insulation to eliminate eddy-current losses in large power windings.

Grain-Oriented Electrical Steel (CRGO)

High-permeability (Hi-B), domain-refined, laser-scribed silicon steel sheets (0.18mm to 0.27mm) for ultra-low no-load loss cores.

Copper & Aluminium Foil / Strip

High-conductivity edge-conditioned copper and aluminium strips for low-voltage distribution and foil windings.

Amorphous Core Alloys

Iron-based metallic glass ribbons offering up to 75% lower core losses compared to conventional CRGO in distribution grids.

Engineering & Technical FAQ

Why is continuously transposed conductor (CTC) essential above 50 MVA?

CTC transposes each individual enamelled copper strand through every position in the conductor cross-section along the winding length. This equalizes flux linkages, drastically cutting circulating eddy currents and winding hot-spot temperatures.
