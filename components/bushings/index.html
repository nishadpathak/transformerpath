Transformer Bushings (HV & LV)

Oil-Impregnated Paper (OIP), Resin-Impregnated Paper (RIP), Resin-Impregnated Synthetics (RIS) & High-Current Bushings

Governing Standards: IEC 60137 IEEE C57.19.00 IEEE C57.19.01 DIN 42530 - 42534
Industry Knowledge Graph Route

Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Voltage Range 1 kV to 1,200 kV AC / 800 kV DC
Current Rating Up to 50,000 A (LV / Generator bushings)
Dielectric Technologies OIP, RIP, RIS, Solid Porcelain, Gas-Insulated (GIS)
Pollution / Creepage Heavy / Very Heavy (Class IV/e per IEC 60815)
Test Standard Power factor / Tan Delta (≤ 0.5% at 20°C), Partial Discharge (≤ 5 pC)

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

OIP Bushings

Oil-Impregnated Paper core with hermetically sealed porcelain or composite insulator; legacy workhorse for bulk EHV applications.

RIP Bushings

Resin-Impregnated Paper dry-type core with silicone composite housing; explosion-proof, moisture-resistant, zero fire hazard.

RIS Bushings

Resin-Impregnated Synthetic mesh with zero cellulose moisture absorption; highest thermal endurance and lifetime reliability.

LV / Generator Bushings

High-current copper stem designs with forced-oil/air/water cooling options for GSU delta connections up to 50 kA.

Direct GIS Connections

SF6-to-oil terminations eliminating exposed overhead clearances in compact substations.
