Transformer Oil, Ester Fluids & Preservation

Applicable Standards: IEC 60296, IEC 61099, ASTM D3487, IEEE C57.147

Engineering & Technical FAQ

What advantages do natural ester fluids provide over mineral oil?

Natural esters have a flash point >300°C (K-class vs O-class for mineral oil at 140°C), are 100% readily biodegradable, and chemically bind moisture to extend cellulose paper insulation life.
