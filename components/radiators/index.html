Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Element Widths 226 mm, 300 mm, 380 mm, 520 mm (DIN 42559)
Length Range 500 mm to 4,000 mm center distance
Cooling Modes ONAN, ONAF, OFAF, ODAF, OFWF
Corrosion Protection Hot-dip galvanized or C5-M high-durability marine epoxy paint
Test Pressure Up to 2.0 bar hydro-tested with zero leakage tolerance

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

Detachable Flanged Radiators

Panel radiators equipped with shut-off valves for on-site dismounting without draining tank oil.

Corrugated Cooling Walls

Automated folded fins for hermetically sealed distribution transformers providing elastic expansion.

Compact OFAF / OFWF Coolers

Tubular and plate-fin heat exchangers for high-density power and locomotive transformers.

Low-Noise Cooling Fans

Aerodynamically optimized impeller fans compliant with stringent environmental decibel limits.
