Engineering & Technical FAQ

What is the difference between OFAF and ODAF cooling?

In OFAF (Oil Forced Air Forced), pumps circulate bulk oil through external coolers. In ODAF (Oil Directed Air Forced), internal baffles guide the forced oil flow directly through winding cooling ducts for maximum heat transfer efficiency.
