Engineering & Technical FAQ

Why is Acetylene (C2H2) considered the most critical DGA gas?

Acetylene is generated only at thermal fault temperatures exceeding 700°C or by high-energy electric arcing. Detection of C2H2 indicates active electrical discharge requiring immediate investigation.
