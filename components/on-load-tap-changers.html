Engineering & Technical FAQ

Why has vacuum OLTC technology become the global utility standard?

Vacuum interrupters extinguish the switching arc inside sealed vacuum bottles, completely avoiding arc contact with insulating oil. This prevents soot, preserves oil dielectric strength, and lowers maintenance cycles.

What is the function of a high-speed transition resistor in an OLTC?

The transition resistor bridges adjacent tap contacts during transfer to maintain continuous load current while limiting circulating current across the bridged winding section.
