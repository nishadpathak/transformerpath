Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Mechanical Protection Buchholz gas/surge relay, Pressure Relief Device (PRD), Sudden Pressure Relay (SPR)
Gauges & Indicators Magnetic Oil Gauge (MOG), Winding (WTI) & Oil (OTI) Temperature Indicators
Online DGA Photoacoustic / NDIR / GC analysis of H2, CH4, C2H2, C2H4, C2H6, CO, CO2, H2O
Dielectric Monitoring Continuous bushing capacitance & Tan Delta dissipation factor sensing
Substation Protocol IEC 61850 MMS & GOOSE, Modbus TCP/RTU, DNP3

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

Buchholz Relays

Detects internal slow gas accumulation (incipient faults) and sudden oil surge (short-circuits) with dual reed switches.

Pressure Relief Devices

Spring-loaded rapid opening valve protecting the transformer tank against catastrophic rupture.

Multi-Gas Online DGA

Continuous automated extraction and gas chromatography for real-time Duval Triangle fault diagnostics.

Integrated Asset Monitors

Unified digital monitoring edge-controllers aggregating DGA, thermal models, bushings, and cooling controls.
