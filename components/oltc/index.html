On-Load Tap Changers (OLTC & DETC)

Vacuum & Oil-Break Tap Changers, Motor Drive Units & Regulating Relays

Governing Standards: IEC 60214-1 IEC 60214-2 IEEE C57.131
Industry Knowledge Graph Route
Product › Supplier › Factory › Country › Direct RFQ

Technical Baseline & Specification Parameters

Switching Technology Vacuum Interrupter (300,000 ops) or Oil-Break Diverter
Max Voltage Up to 1,200 kV system voltage
Max Through-Current Up to 3,000 A per phase
Step Voltage / Positions Up to 35 tapping positions standard
Drive Mechanism Motor Drive Unit (MDU) with SCADA/IEC 61850

Subcategories & Engineering Architectures

Vacuum In-Tank OLTC

Diverter switch inside separate oil chamber utilizing hermetic vacuum bottles to eliminate contact wear and oil carbonization.

Bell-Type / Selector OLTC

Combined selector switch for medium power transformers and industrial furnace applications.

De-Energized Tap Changers (DETC)

Off-circuit rotary or linear tap changers for seasonal or system tap adjustments.

Smart Motor Drive Units

Integrated tap position monitoring, motor current torque analytics, and optical encoder feedback.
