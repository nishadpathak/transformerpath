Verification Standards

The directory is built for buyers who need to trust what they're looking at. Here's exactly what each tier means — and what it doesn't.

The three tiers

Every company in the census sits at one of three levels of confirmation.

Listed

Listed

Free · compiled by us
  • Entry compiled from public sources — company website, public registries, trade records.
  • Name, location, product lines and official link shown as found.
  • Not yet confirmed by the company itself.
  • Any company can claim its entry to move up a tier.
✓ Verified

Verified

$499 / 12 mo
  • A representative of the company has claimed the listing.
  • Contact details, product range and locations confirmed by the company.
  • Working official website and a reachable business contact on file.
  • Carries the ✓ Verified badge across the directory and profile.
★ Supplier Pro

Supplier Pro

$1,499 / 12 mo
  • Everything in Verified, plus priority placement and discovery tools.
  • Priority placement and the ★ Supplier Pro badge.
  • Enhanced listing with expanded factory references and project highlights.
  • Priority RFQ matching and commercial lead routing.

How verification works

1

Claim or submit

A company claims its existing entry or submits a new one through the list / claim form, choosing Verified or Supplier Pro.

2

Confirm the basics

We match the submission against the official website and public records, and confirm a reachable business contact. This clears the Verified tier.

3

Commercial tier review (Supplier Pro)

For Supplier Pro, company profile assets and business registration are reviewed before priority placement is activated.

4

Stay current

Verified entries are kept active by the company. Listings found to be inaccurate are returned to Listed until corrected.

What every listed company is expected to meet

  • Be a real business. A genuine, operating transformer maker, supplier or service company — not a shell or reseller posing as a manufacturer.
  • Represent capability honestly. Product types, ratings and certifications shown should reflect what the company can actually supply.
  • Keep an official channel. A working website or a reachable, monitored business contact.
  • Respect the badge. Verified and Supplier Pro marks are tied to the confirmed listing and may not be reused elsewhere as a general endorsement.
  • Correct what's wrong. Respond to good-faith correction requests so the directory stays trustworthy for buyers.

Commercial tier disclaimer: Supplier Pro is a commercial visibility subscription and does not imply technical certification or endorsement by TransformerPath. Verification confirms identity and stated credentials at the time of review; it is not a guarantee of quality, delivery or commercial performance. Buyers should always carry out their own due diligence before placing orders.
