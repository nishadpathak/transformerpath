Transformer Design & Calculation Workbench

Analytical transformer design workspace (BETA / engineering review) — leakage-field solve and first-pass core/turns/conductor sizing. Outputs are analytical estimates; not a compliance or verification tool.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser — no server call, no AI model in the loop, nothing you enter is ever sent anywhere.

Important. This is an analytical, first-pass design workspace (BETA / engineering review), not a compliance or verification tool. The leakage-field solve and the first-pass core, turns, current, conductor and DC-resistance estimates are analytical — they must be checked against the current published edition of the invoked standard (e.g. IEC 60076) and the judgement of a qualified transformer design engineer before being relied upon. Losses, impedance, thermal, short-circuit and BOM modules remain under engineering review and are not yet released as validated calculations. Where a failure mode is local (hot-spots, stray loss, short-circuit forces), finite-element analysis is required.
