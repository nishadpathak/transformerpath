Middle East Energy 2026 (50th edition)

Completed
Dates 1 Sept 2026 – 3 Sept 2026Venue Dubai World Trade CentreLocation Dubai, UAEOfficial site ↗View on map

The Gulf's flagship power & energy exhibition, held at the Dubai World Trade Centre. Fifty editions of T&D, generation, renewables and smart-grid content make it the region's busiest window for transformer, switchgear and substation D&I — a hard requirement for suppliers selling into the GCC projects boom.

What it covers

Region's biggest energy show — 40,000+ professionals. Power, transmission, distribution, storage, renewables.
