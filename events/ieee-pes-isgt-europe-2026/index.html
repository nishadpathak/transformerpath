About

The IEEE Power & Energy Society's Innovative Smart Grid Technologies (ISGT) conference for Europe, in Budapest. It covers smart grids, grid modernisation, renewables integration and power-system technology — including the transformer, grid-equipment and monitoring content behind the European grid transition.
