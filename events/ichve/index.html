Frequently asked

What is ICHVE?

The IEEE International Conference on High Voltage Engineering, on insulation, dielectric testing and high-voltage phenomena.

Why is it relevant to transformers?

It covers the insulation, partial discharge and high-voltage testing that govern transformer design and reliability.

Who attends?

High-voltage and insulation engineers, researchers and manufacturers, including the transformer insulation community.
