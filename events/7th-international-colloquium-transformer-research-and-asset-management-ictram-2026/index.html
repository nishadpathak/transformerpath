About

The International Colloquium on Transformer Research and Asset Management (ICTRAM), promoted by CIGRE, in Cavtat. It is the most transformer-specific technical gathering — focused on transformer design, condition monitoring, diagnostics, life management and asset management — a leading venue for the transformer engineering and research community.
