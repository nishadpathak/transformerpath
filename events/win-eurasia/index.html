Türkiye's largest industrial & electrical tradeshow, at Istanbul Expo Center. It showcases Eurasia's manufacturing and electrical-economy base — including the substantial Turkish transformer and component OEM network that exports across Europe, Africa and the Middle East.

What it covers

Eurasia's largest industrial fair — Electrotech, Automation, Welding & Energy halls — core platform for transformer OEMs.
