Frequently asked

What is WETEX?

WETEX is Dubai's water, energy, technology and environment exhibition, organised under DEWA and co-located with the Dubai Solar Show.

Why does WETEX matter for transformers?

It covers renewables and grid integration, areas with high transformer, GSU and reactor demand in the Gulf.

Who benefits from attending?

Suppliers of grid, generation, transformer and solar equipment targeting the UAE and wider GCC.
