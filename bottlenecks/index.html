Transformer supply bottlenecks

Five explainers on inputs and factory limits that public sources tie to transformer supply. This series adds no figure of its own.

Compiled 18 October 2026. Dates on each claim are the dates of the linked sources.

What the public record groups together

The review states that there are two major bottlenecks in the large power transformer supply chain: U.S. grain-oriented electrical steel production capacity, and the testing capacity of large power transformer manufacturers. U.S. Department of Energy Electric Grid Supply Chain Review 24 February 2022.

The report states that grain-oriented electrical steel, continuously transposed copper wire, and insulating materials have a significant impact on large power transformer availability and price. U.S. Department of Energy Report to Congress July 2024.

The five explainers

  • Grain-oriented electrical steel
  • Copper and conductors
  • Bushings
  • Insulation pressboard and paper
  • Test bay and factory capacity
