Enterprise Sourcing & Procurement

Category-Driven Sourcing & Factory Prequalification

Structured procurement intelligence across power transformers, distribution units, critical components, and specialized manufacturing equipment. Filter by voltage class, sourced factory capabilities, and geographic footprint, then issue targeted RFQs directly to qualified OEMs.

Open Sourcing Directory → Submit Technical RFQ → Explore Factory Census →
Stage 01

Technical Specification

Define voltage class, MVA envelope, vector group, loss evaluation (A/B factors), insulation system, and site constraints.

Stage 02

Factory Prequalification

Screen confirmed manufacturing facilities (not trading front-ends), test bay envelope, ISO/IEC credentials, and utility track record.

Stage 03

Direct OEM Engagement

Issue structured RFQ packages directly to matched OEM engineering and commercial teams with zero intermediary markup.
