Geo-Chem Middle East

Unclaimed directory listing. The facts below are copied from the company's public website. Checked 25 October 2026.

Independent inspection and testing company. The public site describes transformer-oil condition monitoring: oil sampling, oil quality, dissolved-gas analysis, cellulose-insulation assessment and oil processing.

Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Official website

Location

The contact page gives the Dubai management office as Plot No. 010305, Techno Park, P.O. Box 2209, Jebel Ali, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, telephone +971 4 8867400.

The EIAC certificate published on the site gives the laboratory address as Plot No. TP/010305, National Industries Park, Jebel Ali, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

The same contact page lists a Fujairah office and laboratory at Fujairah Free Zone 1, P.O. Box 3026, Fujairah. The transformer-oil ISO 17025 statement on the site names the Dubai laboratory.

The contact page names the company Geo-Chem Middle East. The EIAC certificate names it GEO CHEM MIDDLE EAST.

Accreditations

The QMS page states that the Dubai laboratory is ISO 17025 accredited by ENAS (Emirates Authority for Standardization and Metrology) for lubricant analysis, transformer oil, gas analysis and aviation fuel analysis, and ISO 17025 accredited by DAC for potable water, waste water and microbiological analysis. It also states that the company is ISO 9001:2008 certified and that inspections follow IFIA requirements.

The profile page states that processes and services are maintained to ISO 9001:2008 and ISO 17025:2005.

The site banner states that a GAC-accredited laboratory in Dubai covers consumer products, and that the Dubai laboratory is accredited to ISO 17025:2005 for cosmetics testing.

The downloads page links certificates it titles as testing (ISO 17025:2017) Dubai, calibration (ISO 17025:2017), ISO 45001:2018 and ISO 14001:2015 for Geo-Chem Middle East, and an ISO 9001 certificate for the Dubai office.

The Dubai testing file is an Emirates International Accreditation Centre certificate, ISO/IEC 17025:2017, number LB-TEST-021, issued to GEO CHEM MIDDLE EAST, with the validity period printed as 26 October 2021 to 13 November 2022. This listing reports that printed period and does not extend it.

Transformer oil tests

The transformer-oil page says condition monitoring covers oil sampling, oil quality, dissolved-gas analysis, cellulose insulation and oil processing.

The published EIAC scope, whose validity is printed to 13 November 2022, names these transformer-oil tests:

  • Kinematic viscosity at 40°C — ASTM D445 and ISO 3104
  • Sulfur by energy-dispersive X-ray fluorescence — ASTM D4294 and ISO 20847
  • Pour point — ASTM D97 and ISO 3016
  • Flash point, Pensky-Martens closed cup — ASTM D93 and ISO 2719
  • Flash and fire points, Cleveland open cup — ASTM D92
  • Density by digital density meter — ASTM D4052 and ISO 12185
  • Density by hydrometer — ASTM D1298 and ISO 3675
  • Interfacial tension of oil against water — ASTM D971
  • Corrosive sulfur — ASTM D1275
  • Potentially corrosive sulphur, covered conductor deposition — ISO 62535
  • Resistivity at 90°C — ASTM D1169 and IEC 60247
  • Colour — ASTM D1500 and ISO 2049
  • Dissipation factor and relative permittivity — ASTM D924
  • Relative permittivity, dielectric dissipation factor and d.c. resistivity at 90°C — IEC 60247
  • Acid number by colour-indicator titration — ASTM D974
  • Acidity by automatic potentiometric titration — IEC 62021-1 and ASTM D664
  • Oxidation stability, including sludge — IEC 61125
  • Water by coulometric Karl Fischer titration — IEC 60814 and ASTM D1533
  • Breakdown voltage, disk electrodes — ASTM D877
  • Breakdown voltage, VDE electrodes — ASTM D1816
  • Breakdown voltage at power frequency — IEC 60156
  • Furanic compounds — ASTM D5837 and ISO 61198
  • Polychlorinated biphenyls — ASTM D4059 and ISO 61619
  • Dissolved gases by gas chromatography — ASTM D3612C and IEC 60567
  • Particle counting — ISO 60970
  • Metals by ICP-AES — ASTM D5185
  • Dibenzyl disulfide — ISO 62697-1
  • Estimated degree of polymerization from 2-furaldehyde, using calculation models named on the scope
