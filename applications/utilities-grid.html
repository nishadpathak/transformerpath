⚡ Transformers for Utilities & Grid

The transmission and distribution backbone. Power, distribution and reactor transformers for TSOs, DSOs and national grids — the scale, voltage and reliability class that governs system security.

Relevant transformer types: PT DT

What Utilities & Grid buyers should look for

  • Voltage class, MVA rating and impedance for the substation role
  • Reactor (shunt/series) options for reactive compensation and line limits
  • Loss and efficiency targets under eco-design / efficiency regulations
  • Type-test, short-circuit withstand and DGA / monitoring provisions
  • Grid-code and interconnection compliance for the operating market
