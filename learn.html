The Design Curriculum

The 26-chapter design core, in the order a real design is built. Each lesson links to its matching volume. This week's spotlight is highlighted.

1 Fundamentals

How a transformer works — mutual induction, the EMF equation, ideal vs real behaviour, and the rating inputs (kVA, voltage ratio, frequency, phases) that drive everything downstream.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

2 Classification & Specification

Power vs distribution, core-type vs shell-type, cooling classes (ONAN/ONAF), vector group, impedance, tap range and the standards (IEC 60076 / ANSI C57) the customer specifies.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

3 Core Design & CRGO

Choosing core area and flux density, CRGO grades and stacking factor, and how silicon steel cuts hysteresis and eddy losses — setting iron weight and no-load loss.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model🧮 Try the design calc

4 Windings & Current Density

Conductor sizing from current density, LV-over-core then HV arrangement, layer/disc/helical windings, and the window-area check that confirms it all fits.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model🧮 Try the design calc

5 Insulation System

Turn, layer and major insulation; pressboard and paper (IEC 60641); clearances and creepage; oil as a dielectric — the discipline that decides reliability and life.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model🧮 Try the design calc

6 Thermal & Cooling Design

Temperature rise, radiators and fans, ONAN/ONAF/OFAF classes, oil flow and hot-spot control — turning loss watts into a safe operating temperature.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model🧮 Try the design calc

7 Tank & Accessories

Tank, conservator, bushings, tap-changer (OLTC/DETC), Buchholz, breather, protection and monitoring — the hardware around the active part.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

8 Dry-Type & Cast Resin

Cast-resin and VPI dry-type transformers — where they win, their thermal classes, and design differences from oil-immersed units.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

9 Worked Design Example

A complete 1000 kVA design worked end-to-end — from spec through core, turns, windings and window check to losses, the way a real designer builds it.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

10 Losses, Impedance & Efficiency

No-load and load loss, leakage reactance and percent impedance, efficiency and voltage regulation — the numbers the customer guarantees.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model🧮 Try the design calc

11 Testing & Type Tests

Routine, type and special tests — ratio, winding resistance, no-load and load loss, impedance, insulation, impulse and temperature-rise — verifying against IEC 60076.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

12 Standards

The IEC 60076 family, ANSI/IEEE C57, and the component standards for bushings, tap-changers, oil and pressboard you design to.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

13 Vector Groups

Dyn11, Ynd1 and the rest — phase displacement, why it matters for paralleling, and how the connection is built in the windings.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

14 Inrush

Magnetising inrush current — why it happens at switch-on, how large it gets, and how protection is set so it doesn't false-trip.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

15 Special Types

Autotransformers, converter and furnace transformers, earthing transformers and other specials — what changes in the design.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

16 Site & Life

Installation, commissioning, oil management, condition monitoring and end-of-life — getting the full service life out of the unit.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

17 Protection

Differential, REF, overcurrent, Buchholz and pressure-relief protection — the relays and devices that keep a transformer (and the grid) safe.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

18 Procurement & Tendering

Reading a tender spec, building a compliant offer, guarantees and penalties, FAT/SAT and how buyers actually evaluate transformer bids.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

19 Glossary & Self-Test

Key terms in one place plus a quick self-test to check your understanding of the whole course.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

20 After-Sales & Service

Warranty, spares, retrofits, oil treatment and field service — the support a transformer needs across decades in operation.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

21 Bushings

OIP/RIP/RIS types, condenser grading, test taps, creepage & pollution class, and why bushings cause most transformer fires — with the 3D condenser-bushing cutaway.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

22 Tap-Changers

OLTC vs DETC, diverter/selector/change-over, resistor vs vacuum switching, AVR control and DGA of the diverter — with the 3D OLTC cutaway.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

23 Impulse & Stress Control

How a winding sees a steep surge: initial vs final distribution, the α constant, and static rings, shields & interleaving that protect the line-end turns.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

24 Fire, Containment & Environment

Pressure/arc protection, nitrogen-injection & deluge, bunds and oil-water separation, ester fire safety and sustainability — the envelope around the transformer.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

25 Worked 500 MVA Design

An EHV 400/220 kV worked design — currents, volts-per-turn, 5-limb core, impedance, impulse grading and ONAN/ONAF/ODAF cooling — behind the 500 MVA 3D model.

📖 Read this section🧩 3D model

26 Engineering Data Appendix

Reference tables designers keep open beside the calculator — ratings, insulation levels, impedance bands, temperature-rise limits and insulating liquids.
